# Green Street Book Swap：基础学生版

原创虚构课堂材料。目标A2，难度由教师复核。先做题，答案由教师另发。

## Before reading

先把词与意思配对：

1. bring：a. 票
2. ticket：b. 带来
3. swap：c. 整理
4. tidy：d. 交换

## Reading text

On Saturday, the Green Street library has a book swap. It starts at ten and ends at twelve. People bring one book in good condition. A worker gives them a ticket. They use it to choose another book. Mia brings a travel book and chooses a cooking book. She wants to make soup with her sister. Jon does not bring a book. He reads for a while, but he does not swap a book. At eleven thirty, workers ask everyone to choose a book. At eleven fifty, they start to tidy the tables. The event is free. The library does not sell books.

## Questions

1. Where is the book swap?
2. What time does the event end?
3. What kind of book does Mia bring?
4. Why does Mia choose a cooking book?
5. True or false: Jon swaps a book. Underline the sentence that supports your answer.

不必逐字抄原文；用简单英语表达，并找出支持答案的句子。
