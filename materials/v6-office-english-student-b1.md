# Green Street Book Swap：提高学生版

原创虚构课堂材料。目标B1，难度由教师复核。先做题，答案由教师另发。

## Before reading

解释以下词或短语在图书交换活动中的意思：condition、receive、exchange、remind。

## Reading text

The Green Street library organises a book swap on Saturday from ten in the morning until twelve. Anyone who brings one book in good condition receives a ticket from a member of staff. The ticket allows the visitor to choose another book from the tables, so visitors need to bring a book if they want to exchange one.

Mia brings a travel book but selects a book about cooking because she wants to learn to make soup with her sister. Although Jon comes without a book, he stays to read for a while. He does not exchange anything. At half past eleven, staff remind everyone to make their choices, and they begin clearing the tables at ten to twelve. The activity is free and no books are sold. For an exchange, visitors need a ticket rather than money.

## Questions

1. How long does the book swap last?
2. What must a visitor bring to receive a ticket?
3. When do staff begin clearing the tables?
4. Why does Mia select a book about cooking?
5. Can visitors buy a book at this event? Give evidence from the text.

答题时用文本证据。讨论题如“你会带哪本书”可以另加，但不要当作有唯一答案的信息题。
